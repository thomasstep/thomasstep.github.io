April was busy, so I did not write my update for that month. I had a bunch of notes written down for May, but I never polished them up and posted them. But in June, I forced myself to write.

I started looking into GCP as an alternative to AWS. Seems fairly similar for basic concepts like VPCs and load balancers, but they lean very heavily on k8s as a compute service. It makes sense given Google created the product. I know they have generic VMs and even a serverless function alternative to Lambda, but overall, everything seems to point to using k8s.

Lately at work, I've been doing a lot of design work. I think I had 3 full designs to go through over the last few months. I'm definitely not complaining about that, though. I feel like helping to design systems is the next rung in my career ladder. I do still code, though, which I appreciate.

Just like most tech companies, we are being asked to learn and use AI tools, and our tool of choice is Claude Code. I've been learning how to use it, but I'm still skeptical. It can punch out a ton of code, but it still doesn't seem completely there. I see it much more as a good boilerplate creator. Once we need nitty-gritty details ironed out, I would rather just do it myself. I imagine most of software engineering job responsibilities are going to be more code review related than writing code. Writing lines upon lines is obviously possible now. Just not polishing it to the level that most professional devs would like.

I am continuing to learn French, which is convenient given a trip over there soon. My wife and I speak it with each other sometimes, but at this point, it's a few broken sentences strung together. It's hard to start at language level 0 and work your way up, but there's also no other way to achieve proficiency. And speaking of languages, I was able to have a long conversation in German the other day at a wedding. One of the other guests came in from Berlin. It was nice to be able to speak for more than a few sentences, although I could definitely feel how much I have lost since I lived there 13 years ago.

Since my last post, I have read Band of Brothers and started on The World for Sale.

I've been out birding, but I haven't seen anything rare lately.
